About the Conversion Practice Game

This game is designed to help you improve your mental math skills and build an intuitive understanding of common measurement conversions. Whether you’re preparing for exams, learning a new skill, or just keeping your brain sharp, practicing conversions can enhance your accuracy and speed in real-world situations.

Why is This Useful?

  • Develops problem-solving skills.
  • Improves real-life application of unit conversions (e.g., cooking, travel, science).
  • Encourages faster mental math.
  • Supports better understanding of metric and imperial systems.

Conversion Formulas

  • Length Conversions

  • Miles to Kilometers: kilometers = miles × 1.60934
  • Kilometers to Miles: miles = kilometers ÷ 1.60934
  • Inches to Centimeters: centimeters = inches × 2.54
  • Centimeters to Inches: inches = centimeters ÷ 2.54
  • Yards to Meters: meters = yards × 0.9144
  • Meters to Yards: yards = meters ÷ 0.9144
  • Meters to Feet: feet = meters × 3.28084
  • Feet to Meters: meters = feet ÷ 3.28084
  • Nautical Miles to Kilometers: kilometers = nautical miles × 1.852
  • Kilometers to Nautical Miles: nautical miles = kilometers ÷ 1.852
  • Nautical Miles to Miles: miles = nautical miles × 1.15078
  • Miles to Nautical Miles: nautical miles = miles ÷ 1.15078

  • Temperature Conversions

  • Celsius to Fahrenheit: Fahrenheit = (Celsius × 9/5) + 32
  • Fahrenheit to Celsius: Celsius = (Fahrenheit - 32) × 5/9
  • Celsius to Kelvin: Kelvin = Celsius + 273.15
  • Kelvin to Celsius: Celsius = Kelvin - 273.15
  • Fahrenheit to Kelvin: Kelvin = (Fahrenheit - 32) × 5/9 + 273.15
  • Kelvin to Fahrenheit: Fahrenheit = (Kelvin - 273.15) × 9/5 + 32

  • Weight Conversions

  • Pounds to Kilograms: kilograms = pounds × 0.453592
  • Kilograms to Pounds: pounds = kilograms ÷ 0.453592
  • Ounces to Grams: grams = ounces × 28.3495
  • Grams to Ounces: ounces = grams ÷ 28.3495
  • Stones to Kilograms: kilograms = stones × 6.35029
  • Kilograms to Stones: stones = kilograms ÷ 6.35029

  • Volume Conversions

  • Gallons to Liters: liters = gallons × 3.78541
  • Liters to Gallons: gallons = liters ÷ 3.78541
  • Cubic Inches to Cubic Centimeters: cubic cm = cubic inches × 16.3871
  • Cubic Centimeters to Cubic Inches: cubic inches = cubic cm ÷ 16.3871
  • Quarts to Liters: liters = quarts × 0.946353
  • Liters to Quarts: quarts = liters ÷ 0.946353
  • Fluid Ounces to Milliliters: milliliters = fluid ounces × 29.5735
  • Milliliters to Fluid Ounces: fluid ounces = milliliters ÷ 29.5735

  • Area Conversions

  • Square Feet to Square Meters: square meters = square feet × 0.092903
  • Square Meters to Square Feet: square feet = square meters ÷ 0.092903
  • Acres to Square Meters: square meters = acres × 4046.86
  • Square Meters to Acres: acres = square meters ÷ 4046.86
  • Square Kilometers to Square Miles: square miles = square km × 0.386102
  • Square Miles to Square Kilometers: square km = square miles ÷ 0.386102

  • Speed Conversions

  • Miles per Hour to Kilometers per Hour: km/h = mph × 1.60934
  • Kilometers per Hour to Miles per Hour: mph = km/h ÷ 1.60934
  • Knots to Miles per Hour: mph = knots × 1.15078
  • Miles per Hour to Knots: knots = mph ÷ 1.15078

  • Time Conversions

  • Seconds to Minutes: minutes = seconds ÷ 60
  • Minutes to Seconds: seconds = minutes × 60
  • Hours to Minutes: minutes = hours × 60
  • Minutes to Hours: hours = minutes ÷ 60
  • Days to Hours: hours = days × 24
  • Hours to Days: days = hours ÷ 24
  • Weeks to Days: days = weeks × 7
  • Days to Weeks: weeks = days ÷ 7
  • Months to Days: days ≈ months × 30.44
  • Days to Months: months ≈ days ÷ 30.44
  • Years to Days: days = years × 365.25
  • Days to Years: years = days ÷ 365.25
