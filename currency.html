Welcome to the Currency Conversion Game!

In this game, you’ll be given a random amount of money in one currency. Your task is to accurately convert it to another currency of your choice as quickly and accurately as possible. Set the game timer as you choose. With the "and reverse" button, you can easily switch between currencies to practice conversions both ways! After the game, you’ll receive an accuracy score to track your progress.

This game is perfect for travelers, as it helps you familiarize yourself with exchange rates and currency conversions, making it easier to do that quick money math while abroad. Happy converting!
